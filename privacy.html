Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Eco Era is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 68-0286784. This page explains what information we receive when you visit ecoera.org or make a gift, and what we do with it. Questions are welcome at support@ecoera.org.

Visiting this site

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive (for example from a search or an ad), the type of device and browser, and an approximate location (country and city) that Google works out from the IP address. We also record when someone clicks a donation button, along with the gift type and amount chosen, so we can see which pages lead to gifts. Google Analytics doesn't give us your name or contact details, and we don't use it for personalized advertising.

Google Analytics uses cookies. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and visitors whose location can't be determined, get no analytics cookies; for them Google receives only basic signals without cookies. When you follow a link between ecoera.org and posadanatura.org, a short identifier is added to the link so the visit counts once.

You can block cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

The site is hosted by Vercel, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Donations

Gifts are processed by Stripe on Stripe's secure checkout pages. Stripe handles your card or bank details, and Eco Era never sees or stores full card numbers. From Stripe we receive your name, email address, billing address if you give one, the amount and date of your gift, and for monthly gifts whether the gift is active. We use this to send receipts and thank-you notes, to keep the records nonprofits are required to keep, and to manage monthly gifts.

If you give through your employer's giving program (such as Benevity) or a donor-advised fund, that organization shares your details with us according to its own policies and the choices you make there.

Email

If you write to us, we keep the correspondence so we can reply and keep track of our work together.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the services that run this site, process gifts and handle our email (Stripe, Google and Vercel), or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to support@ecoera.org. We may need to keep donation records for as long as tax law requires.

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
